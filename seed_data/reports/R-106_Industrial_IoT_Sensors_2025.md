## Page 1: Industrial IoT Sensor Ecosystem Overview
This market study encompasses intelligent wireless and wired sensor nodes deployed across heavy discrete manufacturing, petrochemical refineries, pharmaceuticals, and automotive assembly lines. Sensor modalities include tri-axial vibration accelerometers, ultrasonic acoustics, thermal infrared imaging sensors, and pressure transmitters integrated into edge computing gateways.

## Page 8: Market Valuation & Predictive Maintenance Growth
The global industrial IoT sensors market was valued at USD 12.30 billion in 2024. Driven by predictive maintenance (PdM) workflows that eliminate catastrophic equipment downtime and improve overall equipment effectiveness (OEE), the market is forecasted to grow at a CAGR of 10.1% from 2025 to 2030, reaching USD 19.90 billion by 2030.

## Page 15: Regional Breakdown & Smart Factory Investments
North America accounted for 36% of global industrial IoT sensor revenues in 2024 (USD 4.43 billion), supported by aerospace and semiconductor manufacturing automation. Asia-Pacific followed closely with 34% market share (USD 4.18 billion), experiencing rapid digital transformation across smart factories in Japan, Taiwan, and China. Europe contributed 23% of total revenues.
