## Page 1: Executive Overview & Research Scope
Aquila Insights presents this syndicated industry outlook evaluating global utility adoption of Advanced Metering Infrastructure (AMI) and Automated Meter Reading (AMR) field instrumentation. Important Scoping Note: Unlike broad market studies that bundle recurring cloud software licenses, SaaS subscriptions, and third-party systems integration services into their market sizing, this study strictly focuses on the physical hardware equipment layer—namely water meters, ultrasonic measurement sensors, registers, and radio transceivers.

## Page 4: Utility Procurement Trends & Communication Standards
Across municipal and private concessionaires, procurement cycles have shifted predominantly toward open-standard radio technologies. LoRaWAN and cellular NB-IoT/LTE-M have overtaken proprietary RF mesh architectures in newly commissioned utility tenders. However, municipal budget constraints in emerging regions continue to sustain demand for drive-by/walk-by AMR hardware units as intermediate interim upgrades before full fixed-network AMI deployment.

## Page 9: Hardware Sizing, Historical Trajectory & 5-Year Outlook
The global AMR/AMI water meter hardware market reached a baseline valuation of USD 3.87 billion in 2024. Due to ongoing replacement of mechanical rotary meters with static ultrasonic units, global unit shipments are forecasted to expand at a compound annual growth rate (CAGR) of 9.8% across the 2024 to 2029 forecast window. Note: Aquila Insights models a 5-year outlook ending in 2029, contrasting with studies forecasting through 2030.

## Page 14: Hardware Segment Breakdown & Regional Market Share
In 2024, the hardware-only market stood at USD 3.87 billion worldwide. Asia-Pacific represented the single largest regional volume demand center, capturing 41% of total global hardware revenues, supported by heavy domestic manufacturing capacity and high-density residential installations in China, India, and Southeast Asia. North American shipments followed with 31% hardware share, while Europe comprised 21%.
