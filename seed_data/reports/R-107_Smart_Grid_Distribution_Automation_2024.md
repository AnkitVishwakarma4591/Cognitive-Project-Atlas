## Page 1: Distribution Automation & Substation Scope
Distribution automation (DA) comprises intelligent electronic devices (IEDs), reclosers, automated sectionalizing switches, volt-VAR optimization controllers, and supervisory control and data acquisition (SCADA) interfaces across medium-voltage utility networks.

## Page 6: Market Sizing & Utility Grid Modernization
The global distribution automation market was valued at USD 7.40 billion in 2024. As electric utilities integrate high shares of distributed energy resources (DERs) such as rooftop solar and commercial battery storage, feeder automation spending is anticipated to compound at a CAGR of 7.9% from 2024 to 2029, reaching USD 10.82 billion by 2029.

## Page 12: Regional Dynamics
North America represented the largest market in 2024 with a 40% share (USD 2.96 billion), driven by grid resilience investments against extreme weather outages. Asia-Pacific followed at 33% (USD 2.44 billion), and Europe represented 21%.
