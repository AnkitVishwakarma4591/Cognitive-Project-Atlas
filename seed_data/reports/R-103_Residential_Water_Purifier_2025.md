## Page 1: Executive Summary & Category Definition
The residential water purifier market encompasses point-of-use (POU) and point-of-entry (POE) water treatment appliances designed for single-family residences and multi-family apartments. Purification technologies analyzed include reverse osmosis (RO), ultraviolet (UV) disinfection, ultrafiltration (UF), and gravity-based filtration systems. Rising global consumer awareness regarding microplastics, PFAS ("forever chemicals"), heavy metals, and industrial effluents in municipal tap water continues to drive residential adoption worldwide.

## Page 6: Global Market Valuation & Growth Forecast
The global residential water purifier market was valued at USD 9.60 billion in 2024. Driven by rapid urbanization in emerging economies and retrofitting of smart, IoT-enabled countertop systems in developed regions, the market is projected to expand at a CAGR of 8.5% from 2025 to 2030, reaching USD 15.54 billion by 2030.

## Page 11: Regional Dynamics & Asia-Pacific Dominance
Asia-Pacific accounted for 46% of total global residential water purifier revenues in 2024, representing USD 4.42 billion. China and India remain the two highest volume markets globally due to groundwater contamination challenges and the expansion of the middle-class consumer demographic. North America followed with 24% market share, dominated by undersink RO filtration systems with smart filter replacement indicators.
